## Who We Are

**cyberwirez** is a Philippine-based cybersecurity community built by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts. We compete in CTF competitions globally — on platforms like HackTheBox, TryHackMe, and other international CTF events — and we run regular meetups to grow the local cybersecurity scene.

We crack it. We own it.

## What We Do

- **CTF Competitions** — We compete as a team in global Capture The Flag events across HackTheBox, TryHackMe, and other international platforms
- **HackTheBox Meetups** — We organize regular HTB-focused sessions where members tackle machines, share techniques, and learn together
- **Knowledge Sharing** — Talks, presentations, and discussions covering real-world cybersecurity topics — from reverse engineering to threat intelligence
- **Community Building** — We welcome Filipino aspiring cybersecurity professionals at all skill levels, whether you're just starting out or already deep in the field
- **Games & Challenges** — Sometimes we just play and have fun with it
